2. Two ways to log the same result

A · Inline: stay in the workout card. B · Focused sheet: enter the same fields in a separate panel, then return. Both retain format-specific progress, optional effort, notes and applicable weights.

3. One framed counter, two sizes

Both options keep minus, quantity and plus inside one frame. Blank means unreported; zero is an entered result. Compare the compact control with the roomier version. These are independent of the logging-panel choice.
