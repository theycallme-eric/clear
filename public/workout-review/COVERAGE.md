## What the examples do not certify

- F02 hidden-value migration/completion semantics remain a backend/requirements decision; the two-step UI uses held fixture answers.
- F05/F18 prepared training context, refresh and policy are not running in this prototype. The report is controlled display data, not a second calculation engine.
- F07/F17 numeric-range/guide storage, versioning, reload/repeat compatibility and failure recovery need the real contracts. Rendering a proposed reader does not prove a migration or persistence path.
- F12/F13/F15 workout-quality, duration and conditioning intensity outcomes need their own future generation evidence. Existing format fixtures exercise UI, not a new workout corpus.
- F14 uses actual providers/writers with in-memory clients. That is stronger than the older synthetic controller, but not hosted persistence or physical-phone proof.
- F19 presentation is not authority to delete saved preferences. F20 runtime/backward-client/backend failures outside these named states remain integration obligations.
