Contrast Audit
Every published ratio, measured at load from the tokens as they currently resolve — not quoted from a table. Each pair is composited the way the browser paints it (opaque ground, translucent surface over it, text over that) and read back as pixels, because color-mix(in oklab, …) and sRGB arithmetic differ by enough to cross a threshold. Change a token and this card tells you what broke.
Measuring…
Thresholds: 7:1 normal text and 4.5:1 large (≥24px) for AAA, which only Mono claims; 4.5 / 3 for AA, the target for the three colour skins. Disabled text is exempt under WCAG and is not audited. Atmospheric and decorative layers are deliberately not held to a threshold.
