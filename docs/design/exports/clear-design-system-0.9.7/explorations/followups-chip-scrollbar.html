2 · Where the scrollbar sits

You want it thin and in the structure colour, without taking up space or covering content. Those two can pull against each other, so here are three ways to handle it. Scroll each one.
ABrowser default
Whatever the browser does. On some systems it overlays the content, on others it takes a strip of width and pushes the rows narrower.
BIn the frame's padding
The bar sits in the gap between the rows and the frame, which is reserved either way, so rows never shift or get covered.
Recommended: native, and never covers or moves content
CDrawn rail on the edge
A 2px system-drawn rail on the frame's inside edge, in the margin. Most panel-like, but it can't be dragged, so it's an indicator rather than a control.
