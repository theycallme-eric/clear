You're right, and it isn't just the test pages. Every frame paints a solid dark ground under its tint, so the atmosphere only shows in the gaps between cards. I added that on purpose so a 10% tint always reads as 10%, but it means frames block the background completely. Also, my earlier test pages used a stand-in gradient. These use the real atmosphere layer.

Only the ground under the frames changes. Contrast is measured over the brightest part of the atmosphere, which is the worst case for reading.
