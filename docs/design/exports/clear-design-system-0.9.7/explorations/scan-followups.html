4 · Flicker when a chip is clicked

Clicking a chip plays the interlace flicker. Interlace means "the display re-synced after a state change". Click each chip.
AInterlace on toggle (today)
The 100ms toggle timing plus a 100ms flicker on every toggle.
BHard cut
The state changes with the normal 100ms toggle timing, the same as a checkbox. No flicker. Interlace stays for content that updates on its own.
Recommended: a click already shows the change
