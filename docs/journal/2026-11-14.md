## What changed

- **Round 7 mounted at the app root.** `PRODUCT_PRESENTATION` (`src/ui/product-presentation.ts`) holds the approved
  look choices only. `AppRuntime` mounts it, and so does the test `AppProviders`, so tests see what users see.
- **No sample data in the product.** The presentation's data-bearing inputs are optional, and the product leaves
  them out:
  - warm-up/cooldown `guides`;
  - `suggestedLoads` (the prescription's own load shows);
  - the timed `saveResult` (timed blocks keep their shipped renderers until the timed tasks);
  - the training report;
  - the onboarding preview draft.
  - The gallery passes sample values through `GallerySamplePresentation`.
- **Behaviour kept under the look** (a presentation does not authorize behaviour changes):
  - Settings Goal/Experience;
  - every History filter;
  - full Onboarding.
- **Owner decisions** (Eric, 2026-11-13, chat: "Accept recommendations"):
  - Accepted as Round 7 behaviour:
    - set logging as a dialog;
    - the compact section bar;
    - Summary feedback in a dialog;
    - Generate's Goal/Focus summary without the Place/Notes helpers;
    - format-only badges;
    - History row order.
  - Kept:
    - Generate's intensity explanation;
    - the coaching empty-state messages.
- **Section heading.** The compact bar draws no title. Every block's group header names the section, except the
  older AMRAP renderer. Under Round 7 it now draws the same `MovementGroupHeader`, so focus after Next lands on a
  heading. A first attempt added a hidden heading to the bar instead. That duplicated the visible block heading,
  and it was removed.
- **Accessibility:** movement headings with inline equipment were named "back squatbarbell". They are now named by
  the movement, and the equipment describes the heading.
- **Bug fixes found by the test rewrite:**

    "logged", and the round keeps what was typed.

    confirmation from the card. The card now opens the next entry, and the same rest keeps running inside the
    dialog.
