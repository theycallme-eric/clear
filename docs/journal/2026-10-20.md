**Observed cause (measured, not inferred).** In every row: the `.clr-check` wrapper is 300px wide (x=50..350; the `clr-stack` fieldset stretches it). The 20px drawn box is at x=50..70. The transparent 44px input (`pointer: coarse` size) is at x=178..222, because `foundation.css:740` centres it on the wrapper with `left: 50%; top: 50%`. `document.elementFromPoint` at the box centre returns `span.clr-check__box` or `span.clr-check__dash`. Those spans are aria-hidden siblings of the input and the label, not inside the `<label>`, so a press there reaches no control. The input's hit area covers neither the box nor, for most labels, the label text (labels span x=78..~180).
