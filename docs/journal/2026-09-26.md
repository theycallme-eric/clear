## Intended outcome

Per-slot swap on Review with up-to-3 history and undo, unit swap for blocks, a regenerate nudge on
the third swap, `ErrorState` on failure, and every accepted swap persisted as a revision with
lineage before the workout starts (D6).

## The design decision the requirement forces, recorded because it is not obvious

`docs/specs/generation/exercise-swap.md` says swap state is **local only** and "no database writes
until Start". REQ-044's own acceptance criteria say the opposite — "persisted as a revision with
lineage before the workout starts" and "swap survives reload" — and they win: the spec predates
D6's fix. REV-02's `generate-section` takes a `session_id` and writes through
`swap_session_exercise` / `swap_session_block`, so a swap on Review is only possible against a
session that already exists. `swap_session_block`'s own gate admits `prescribed` as well as
`active`, so revising a reviewed-but-unstarted session is supported by the schema as written.

The intended screen behaviour, which is **not yet built**: Review persists the composition on the
*first swap* (`accept`, leaving it `prescribed`), swaps against it, and Start then only calls
`start`. Regenerate must `abandon` a session that was persisted, and the discard dialog's "this
workout has not been saved" copy must become conditional — it is false once a swap has been made.

Counting swaps must be derived from lineage rather than from a separate in-memory ledger, so it
survives a reload: walk the `slot_id` chain, maintaining a stack of exercise ids; a revision whose
exercise equals the entry below the top of the stack is an **undo** (pop), anything else is a
**swap** (push). Swaps used is the number of pushes and is monotonic — three swaps lock the slot
whether or not they were undone, which is what the spec's `swapCount` means. The stack below the
top is the history, and it is bounded at 3 by the swap limit rather than by a separate rule.
