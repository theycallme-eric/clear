Decisions and assumptions:

- Enforcement is in the three functions, not a trigger. A direct PostgREST write to
  `location_equipment` or `locations` under the owner-only policies is not evaluated; the app makes
  none. A trigger would also fire on account-deletion cascades and was judged out of scope.
- `save_location` evaluates only when the location is new or its stored equipment differs from what
  it held, so a rename of a location saved before this rule is not refused for equipment it did not
  touch. `set_default_location` always evaluates the target.
- Deleting a location that is not the last changes nothing generation reads and is not evaluated.
  Deleting the last one is evaluated as the empty list and refused as `missing_equipment`. This
  changes SET-02's earlier allowance for a user with no locations; `deletionRefusal` (client) is
  unchanged and the server's refusal rolls the optimistic removal back.
- A caller with no profile row is not evaluated. `complete_onboarding` is untouched (another task).
- The refusal reaches the athlete through the existing failed-save toast (`useInlineSave` →
  `showErrorToast`), which already shows `error.message` and already rolls back.
