Branch: `agent-runner/task-013-a1-ecbc1779`. The goal was REQ-008 / JOURNEY-013. Review should show
a suggested weight per anchored exercise with its session-count confidence, open a `Dialog`
explaining last session's sets, the recorded RPE and the rule that fired, and take a per-session
override that never rewrites the anchor. It must show nothing where there is no anchor, and keep
the briefing usable while the anchor reads load or fail.
