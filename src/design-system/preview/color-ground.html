Colors — Ground
Each skin sets its own near-black base and near-white ink. The base is tinted toward that skin's interaction hue, which is why no two grounds are the same neutral — and why a hardcoded #171717 is CLEAR's value, not the system's.

The background never goes lighter than 900, and every surface in the system is an alpha tint over it rather than a separate opaque colour — which is what makes a skin five hues instead of forty. Read --base and --ink, never the literals.
