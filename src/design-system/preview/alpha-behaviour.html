Goes olive
The failure is not brightness, it is naming. Chartreuse is only chartreuse at high lightness \u2014 darkened it becomes olive, a different colour with different associations. Violet, emerald, mint and CLEAR's own blue are recognisable across the whole lightness range, so they tint without changing identity. Structure hues are exempt: they appear at full strength as borders and at 10% as a whisper, never in the middle. It is the interaction role, which lives at 40\u201360%, that must survive.
