Contained scroll: edge options
Three ways to handle the pinned layer now that the edge is a hard rule instead of a fade. Scroll each one. While you scroll, a streak lights up along the top and bottom edges and settles once you stop. It works the same in all three. The difference is what happens to content once it reaches the layer.
