Focus vs selected
You want focus and selected kept separate. This shows B and D in the cases where that's hardest: a control that's already selected, and a control whose border is already bright. Check the Focused + selected column, then switch to Mono, where every role is the same grey. The readout under B measures whether focus actually changes the border enough to see: 3:1 is the minimum.
Skin
BBorder lights up
Focus and selected both change the same border, so they compete for one signal. On a selected chip and on the primary button, focus has to be told apart by a small shift in colour, and in Mono by brightness alone.
DCorner brackets
Focus is a shape, not a colour. The brackets sit outside the control, so they never touch its border or surface, and they read the same on a selected control, a bright control, and in every skin.
