# Process journal

For every material work session, maintain `docs/journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md` as an append-only,
reviewable account of how the work happened. A question-only or read-only orientation session
does not require an entry.

Record at meaningful checkpoints and before handoff:

- the intended outcome and the issue, branch, or pull request involved;
- what changed locally and what changed in GitHub or another external service;
- failures, misleading signals, and their actual causes;
- corrections made, especially changes that prevent recurrence;
- verification performed and the exact result;
- unresolved questions, assumptions, and the next safe action.

Prefer a concise factual narrative over a minute-by-minute transcript. Never record secrets,
tokens, one-time codes, private email contents, or personal data. Commit relevant journal updates
with the work they describe.

Follow the full convention in `docs/process/AGENT_PLAYBOOK.md` §10.
